About the Registry

The Australian Pluripotent Stem Cell Registry aims to build assurance in the cell line resources underpinning Australian stem cell research, by creating unambiguous digital identification and curated descriptions for every Australian stem cell line.

Authoritative Source

The Australian Pluripotent Stem Cell Registry (the Registry) is the authoritative source of information about Australian-made stem cell lines. We provide each stem cell line with a unique, persistent digital identifier that protects and preserves its provenance—from donor and generating facility to research laboratory and the broader Australian community.

The Registry links each stem cell line with information about how it was made and by whom. We bridge information silos by connecting research data to the cell line of origin. By making this information discoverable and trustworthy, our aim is to support responsible uptake, sharing and translation of Australian stem cell research.

Our Partners

Enabling Openness in Australian stem cell Research (EOAR)

The Enabling Openness in Australian stem cell research is a project that brings together social scientists, philosophers, legal and ethics scholars together with digital specialists and stem cell researchers. EOAR is interested in the mechanisms that build trust in stem cell research and stem cell therapies, seeking to understand how Australian medical researchers find and access human stem cell resources. The registry was established in response to this research.
