Overview

Description

This induced pluripotent stem cell line was reprogrammed from fibroblasts from a female with neurodevelopmental disorder with epilepsy, spasticity and brain atrophy. The line carries a homozygous splice-site variant in TRAPPC4. Expression of pluripotency markers and directed differentiation into endoderm, mesoderm and ectoderm have been reported.
