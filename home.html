The Australian Pluripotent Stem Cell Registry

We provide a trusted foundation for Australian stem cell research by ensuring the unambiguous identification, discoverability and ethical reuse of pluripotent stem cell lines derived from Australian donors. We aim to build assurance in the resources underpinning Australian stem cell research, increase visibility and support collaboration with Australia's stem cell research community.
